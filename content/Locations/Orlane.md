This once prosperous farming town now lay dying at the outskirts of  [[New Barovia]]. It rests nestled between the [[Twilight Forrest]] to its North and the [[Krymoor]] swamp lands to the South. It's a few days travel to the East of [[Winterhaven]] and just South of to the village of [[Hommlet]].
