New Barovia is what the dread lord [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] has called these new lands he finds himself in. He knows not how he got here, though many of the locals are still familiar to him. Here his word is law, and he is a dreadful ruler. The people here detest this "man", often referring to him as "the devil Strahd" followed by spitting at the ground. Many make do with the dreadful state of affairs, though when the mists rolled in along with it came many great beasts and monsters who now stalk the roads and the shadows.
