The taint is a concept from the *Heroes of Horror* supplement book put out for *Dungeons and Dragons 3.5 Edition*. In it, when characters interact with extreme evil and horrific events, the build corruption, known as taint. This was based on a very similar system used in *The Legend of the 5 Rings*.

For this campaign, some modifications and adaptations must be made in order to work both from a story and 4e perspective.

# Corruption and Depravity
When a character interacts with evil, be in an object, monster, location, etc, they will gain either a **Corruption Point** or a **Depravity Point**. The specifics on gaining points will be discussed further down.

**Corruption** is related to a characters **Constitution**. Any time a character gains more **Corruption Points** than their **Constitution** modifier the must make a saving throw. On a failure, the character is **Weakened** for one round.

**Depravity** is related to a character **Wisdom**. Any time a character gains more **Depravity Points** than their **Wisdom** modifier they must make a saving throw. On a failure, the character is **Dazed** for one round.
# Tainted Characters
As characters explore the campaign world they win inevitably encounter great evils and disturbing displays of debauchery.

Tainted character will eventually gain access to exclusive feats and powers, however that is still in a *WIP* stage. For now it is mostly a negative effect.

A characters symptoms and severity of corruption is determined by the amount of points they posses compared to their Wisdom and Constitution scores. Low Constitution having characters will exhibit more physical marks of corruption, while low Wisdom having characters will develop more mental effects. See the table below. Use **Corruption Points** with **Constitution**, and use **Depravity Points** with **Wisdom**.

| Con/Wis Score | Mild Taint | Moderate Taint | Severe Taint | Dead/Insane |
| ------------- | ---------- | -------------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| 1-4           | 1          | 2-5            | 6-13         | 14+         |
| 5-8           | 1-5        | 6-13           | 14-27        | 28+         |
| 9-12          | 1-7        | 8-19           | 20-41        | 42+         |
| 13-16         | 1-9        | 10-25          | 26-55        | 56+         |
| 17-20         | 1-11       | 12-31          | 32-69        | 70+         |
| 21-24         | 1-13       | 14-37          | 38-83        | 84+         |
| 25-28         | 1-15       | 16-43          | 44-97        | 98+         |
Once a threshold has been reached, the character must have a ***Remove Affliction*** ritual performed on them and successfully remove points below the threshold. For example, once a character gains their first point of **Depravity** or **Corruption** they need to have *Remove Affliction* performed on them until they are free of points. If the character crosses the threshold in **Moderate Taint** then they must get into **Mild Taint** levels again within 24 hours.

## Deities and Tainted Characters
A character who worships any non evil god will lose the favor of their deity should they reach **Severe Taint** in either category. Warnings will be sent at lower levels of taint.

If a character loses the favor of their god through taint levels, they will be claimed by [[Orcus]] regardless of the characters wishes. He will allow them to continue using their divine powers through his corruption, and may even force their hands to commit evil deeds in his name. Hope is not lost, as a character who manages to cleans themselves of their taint and makes amends may very well be accepted into the service of good again. However, achieving such a task is no small feat, and may come a great a sacrifice.

# Becoming Tainted
Generally, a character does not want any taint, but it may be unavoidable. In this games lore, taint is spiritual and manifests in the soul much like clerical abilities. When [[Orcus]] crusaded through these lands, in his wake the taint of the pits spread.

## Environmental
Places of great evil may bestow corrupting taint upon the character by simply entering the area. When entering such a tainted place, characters gain **1 point** of **Depravity** or **Corruption** depending on the environment. In rare cases, places of exceptional evil may bestow a number of points equal to **1d4**. 

For every 24 hours a character remains in such a location, the GM makes a **Attack: +1 vs. Will**. This attack gets +2 in exceptionally evil locations, and stacking +1 for ever consecutive 24 hours the characters remain in the location. On a hit, the character gains **1 point**. On a critical, they gain **2 points**. On a failure, nothing happens. If the characters leave the location and return, so long as they spent at least 24 hours outside of the location, the bonuses to the roll are reset.

**Countermeasures:** Anyone affected by an **aura** ability with the **divine** keyword originating from a good aligned individual gets a **+3** to their **Will** defenses for this hazard only.

### Powers in Evil Environments
Corruption through exposure is not the only worry in these places. Anyone using a power with the ***Shadow, Nethermancy, Necromancy and/or, Necrotic*** keywords will have a **+1** to hit *and/or* damage. Likewise, any burst effects from such powers are also increased by 1. However, using such powers also forces the character to make a **saving throw**. On a failure, the character gains **1 point** of **Corruption** if the power is a weapon based attack; otherwise they gain **1 point** of **Depravity**. All qualifying powers cause the saving throw even if they don't have an attack roll, damage roll, or burst effect.

### Death in Tainted Lands
Any creature that dies within tainted lands will rise within **1d4** hours as an undead, usually a [[Zombie]].

### Cleansing the World
In such places of evil, it is possible to cleanse the land. By maintaining a ***Hallowed Temple*** ritual for 24 hours in a place of evil, the land will be reclaimed by the forces of good. This can only be preformed by a good aligned individual who has devoted themselves to a non-evil deity. Should the ritual be interrupted, the process would need to start over. 

**Note:** doing so to cleanse the lands means preforming the ritual within evil territory, and the powers overwhelm the otherwise safe sanctuary normally created by the ritual. Anyone is able to cross the threshold of the temple in such cases, and many will in an attempt to slay or disrupt the holly individual responsible for the ritual.

# Resisting and Healing Taint
Taint is not a one way street, though reversing its effects are difficult and can become impossible over time. 

## Taint Absorbing Items
One of the most common methods of resisting the taint. Talismans, object of pure jade, warding glyph carved totems, all have been used with success for warding off the taint. Any item created to absorb taint costs at minimum **100 gp** to craft. In some towns, spiritual folks may create and sell such items to travelers wishing to stave off the shadows on their travels. Each object can absorb **7 points** of corrupting taint from any source. Multiple items can protect an individual more, though corrupting energies will work harder to destroy such instances of retaliation. See the table below.
