In a manor overlooking the harbor, the lady of the house was in labor. This was to be her first born, and the heir to the family fortune. Long had they failed to conceive, and it would all be over in mere moment. The nurse stocked her hand as the doctor guided the child out from its home. The stench of blood and sweat filled the air, wafting its way down the halls where the lord sat nervously waiting. Minutes turned to hours, the the night grew long. At the stroke of midnight, a blood chilling scream echoed through these halls. The lord rushed to his wife, terrified as to what had occurred. In her arms, wrapped in blue was a screaming baby boy. All who gazed upon the babe had a look of dread to them. When the lord approached, his wife pulled away in fear. He gently grabbed her weakened arm and pulled close to himself, so that he may see the child in the light. Pale red like pressed rose petals. Tiny horns peaking up from the forehead. This was no baby, it was a devil. It was a tiefling.

Tieflings are one of the more obscure results of the [[Cataclysm]]. No one knows why or how they came to be. Arcane scholars who've had the chance to examine them closer have discovered trace signs of abyssal blood in some, and infernal blood in others. Those who bare the curse are usually not trusted by many, though there is nothing inherently evil about tieflings, despite their heritage. Many, in fact, are quite virtuous heroes.
