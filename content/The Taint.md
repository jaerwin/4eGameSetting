The taint is a concept from the *Heroes of Horror* supplement book put out for *Dungeons and Dragons 3.5 Edition*. In it, when characters interact with extreme evil and horrific events, the build corruption, known as taint. As their taint score increases, the more they reflect the physical and mental strain of the world, eventually leading to the death or insanity of the character. This was based on a very similar system used in *The Legend of the 5 Rings*.

For this campaign, some modifications and adaptations must be made in order to work both from a story and 4e perspective.

# Gaining Taint and its Consequences
Generally, a character does not want any taint, but it may be unavoidable. In this games lore, taint is spiritual and manifests in the soul much like clerical abilities. When [[Orcus]] crusaded through these lands, in his wake the taint of the pits spread.

## Environmental
Places of great evil may bestow corrupting taint upon the character by simply entering the area. When entering such a tainted place, characters gain **1 taint point**. In rare cases, places of exceptional depravity may bestow a number of points equal to **1d4**. 

For every 24 hours a character remains in such a location, the GM makes a **Attack: +1 vs. Will**. This attack gets +2 in exceptionally evil locations, and stacking +1 for ever consecutive 24 hours the characters remain in the location. On a hit, the character gains **1 taint point**. On a critical, they gain **2 taint points**. On a failure, nothing happens. If the characters leave the location and return, so long as they spent at least 24 hours outside of the location, the bonuses to the roll are reset.

**Countermeasures:** Anyone affected by an **aura** ability with the **divine** keyword originating from a good aligned individual gets a **+3** to their **Will** defenses for this hazard only.

### Shadow Powers
Corruption through exposure is not the only worry in these places. Anyone using a power with the ***Shadow, Nethermancy, Necromancy and/or, Necrotic*** keywords will have a **+1** to hit *and/or* damage. Likewise, any burst effects from such powers are also increased by 1. However, using such powers also forces the character to make a **saving throw**. On a failure, the character gains 1 taint point. All qualifying powers cause the saving throw even if they don't have an attack roll, damage roll, or burst effect.

### Death in Tainted Lands
Any creature that dies within tainted lands will rise within **1d4** hours as an undead, usually a [[Zombie]].

### Cleansing the World
In such places of evil, it is possible to cleanse the land. By maintaining a ***Hallowed Temple*** ritual for 24 hours in a place of evil, the land will be reclaimed by the forces of good. This can only be preformed by a good aligned individual who has devoted themselves to a non-evil deity. Should the ritual be interrupted, the process would need to start over. 

**Note:** doing so to cleanse the lands means preforming the ritual within evil territory, and the powers overwhelm the otherwise safe sanctuary normally created by the ritual. Anyone is able to cross the threshold of the temple in such cases, and many will in an attempt to slay or disrupt the holly individual responsible for the ritual.
