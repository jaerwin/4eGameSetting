Once the proud [[Dragon Knights]] of [[Good King Cyrus]], now mostly master-less wanderers; forced into mercenary work just like most who travel. Many of the new lords have bounties on these folk, for they represent hope and the fortune that once ran freely through the realm. Some have capture orders, recognizing that putting such symbols to work as slaves or servants under their control is a greater show of power. Others simply have a kill bounty, not caring about their long term survival.

It is also said that the [[Cult of Tiamat]] has discovered an ancient pact in order to become one with the dragon, much like their vile goddess. Perhaps the same can be done with the good god, [[Bahamut]].

# Playing a Dragonborn
Dragonborn tend to be prideful warriors. Masters of combat, both on and off the battlefield. Now, their kind is hunted everywhere they go, causing them to be rather reserved in who they are open with. Playing one of these folk it to embody the dragon heritage and the refugee lifestyle. Perhaps you were once a [[Dragon Knights|dragon knight]] yourself, or your parent was and they taught you everything you need to know.

## Possible Goals
* Discover what became of the ancient knights order.
* Discover your heritage.
* Take revenge on those who've wronged you.
