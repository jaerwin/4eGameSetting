The pixies have been travelling between the fey wilds and the mortal realms since the march of time began. They act as masterful scouts for members of the court. Spry, outgoing, and able to squeeze into places most can only dream. Their insectoid like wings come in a number of translucent colors, looking like that of stained glass. Some wise men believe one can see the fate of the cosmos in the wings of a pixie.

# Playing a Pixie
As a pixie, players will have a number of strengths and weaknesses that are entirely unique. Most will likely want to take a striker class of some kind to take advantage of their abilities. Pixies tend to be mischievous but empathetic by nature. However, pixies of the [[Unseelie Court]] tend to be cruel and are quite relentless in their "pranks".

## Possible Motivations
* Trying to find your way back to your court.
* Carving your own destiny, trying to find a place in the dark mortal realms.
* Actively sent to spy for a powerful fey.
