Strahd Von Zarovich, the first vampire and the ancient ruler of [[Barovia]]. Many moons ago he was thwarted by a group of adventurers who were ensnared by his lands mists. Upon his death, his soul was cast out into the void, supposedly banished from these realms. Unknown time had passed and the dread lord found himself awakening alone in a field of tall grass. The pale moonlight glistened across his sweat soaked snowy skin. His body lifted with the creaks and pops of time snapping him back into reality. This was not the lands he had once known, this was a new conquest for the dread lord to undertake.
# Early Life
Little is known about the dread lords pre-vampiric days. He was born of noble blood, and he had conquered the land of [[Barovia]] in his families name. It is said that when his family made the trip to reside in [[Castle Ravenloft]] that they brought two individuals who would spell doom for the young prince. The first, his younger brother, [[Sergei Von Zarovich]], who Strahd had a long rivalry with. The second, the woman who captured the young mans heart, [[Tatyana]]. Alas, Strahd's love would go unrequited. It was Sergei who Tatyana would choose, and their wedding was sealed. This drove the young Strahd mad with rage. It's said that he prayed to the dark gods of the land, and with them he formed a blood pact. On the day of the wedding, Strahd killed Sergei to claim Tatyana as his own. The poor girl fled in horror, he white dressed now stained with the blood of her lover, and the vile murdered gave chase. In desperation, she flung herself from the cliff face overlooking the sea, never to be found.

For months, Strahd grieved alone in his now empty castle. When next he made his face known, his pact was complete. Vampyr is what he was now. A creature of death, stalker of the night. Even still, he never fully stopped searching for the love of his life, Tatyana.
# Barovian Ruler
The count was forced to continue ruler over [[Barovia]] for countless centuries, trapped by a magical mist that kept all who entered stuck in the lands. He would spend most of his waking life trying to find some means of escape.
# Modern Day
Strahd, having been defeated in his home lands for the final time, awoke in these lands much to his surprise. To what end did the [[Dark Powers]] who kept him trapped for so long now decided to finally rid himself of his home land. These lands he found to be in decline already, not unlike where he had been. Using his thousands of years of knowledge as a predator of the knight and an old conqueror, he made his way through the lands until he managed to obtain an audience with [[Good King Cyrus]]. He would spend the next few years corrupting the king, breaking the country from the shadows of aristocracy. Once much of the damage had been done, and the armies had turned to the side of the count, Strahd would slay Cyrus before the generals and crown himself king of [[New Barovia]].

In the years that followed, the old castle would twist and bend itself into the shape of old [[Castle Ravenloft]], as if the spirits that made it before now haunt Strahd to this day.
