# The Setting
The world is in decline. Some centuries ago, a great battle between [[Orcus]] and [[The Raven Queen]] took place. No on is sure about the details, as information from that time period mostly lost; with what little remains of it also being muddied by bias. Once great empires and kingdoms now lay in ruin, ruled over by the dread lords who control with wicked powers granted to them by their dread masters.

# Rules and Guidelines
The setting is going for a Gothic horror vibe (see the [[Appendix N]] for some more details), which means the list of playable features is rather curated. Players are allowed to select from the classes and races outlined to them, and nothing else. Of course, 4e is a very heroic game where the players are a cut above the common swill. This could clash with the tone, but it is trusted players will create with their game environment in mind. Each should take care to read the brief preface given for each race and class to understand how it differs from what is written in the books. Should you have any questions your DM will be happy to answer.

Of course, it would be impossible to curate everything, so here are a few extra rules players are expected to follow:
1. Use the standard array (Method 1) found in the Player's Handbook for starting attributes. Point buy may become available much later (lets at least reach level 10)
2. If the source is from a Dragon or Dungeon Magazine article, you must as for approval. This likely won't be a problem the majority of the time, but it's better to be safe.
