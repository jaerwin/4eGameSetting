# The Setting
The world is in decline. Some centuries ago, a great battle between [[Orcus]] and [[The Raven Queen]] took place. No on is sure about the details, as information from that time period mostly lost; with what little remains of it also being muddied by bias. Once great empires and kingdoms now lay in ruin, ruled over by the dread lords who control with wicked powers granted to them by their dread masters.

The players will be positioned in a world overrun with demons, devils, and the undead. Cosmic beings battle for supremacy in a nearly ruined world.

# Rules and Guidelines
This section is mandatory reading for my players. You need not worry too much about links to other pages, as all the information necessary should be contained here unless otherwise noted.

## Player Buy-in
The setting is going for a Gothic horror vibe (see the [[Appendix N]] for some more details). Players are expected to keep the vibe to the best of their ability, both pre-game and during.
Players should expect the following:
1. High lethality - The first boss in particular will be a major test of this.
2. Simplified travel, but with a higher focus on area exploration.
3. Possibly dark subjects.
4. Combat and Dungeon Crawling!
5. Large amounts of undead!

## Character Creation
Players are allowed to select from the classes and races outlined to them, and nothing else. Each should take care to read the brief preface given for each race and class to understand how it differs from what is written in the books. Should you have any questions your DM will be happy to answer.

Please follow the following guides:
1. Use the standard array (Method 1) found in the Player's Handbook for starting attributes. Point buy may become available much later (lets at least reach level 10)
2. If the source is from a Dragon or Dungeon Magazine article, you must as for approval. This likely won't be a problem the majority of the time, but it's better to be safe.

## Party Balance
This is an aside that may not affect us much. Most everything in this edition is balanced around a party of 5. At the moment, we have at least 3 members who seem enthusiastic about play.
Regardless, if the party ends up being less than 5 I will add a single NPC to the party to fill in the gap. This will ensure at *minimum* a party of 4, something easy mod adjust for.

This is **not** a DMNPC. In fact, the party is probably going to build it's sheet together. One person will be selected to run it in combat, and I'll only do the roleplay.
The NPC will **not** be a DM mouthpiece. The NPC will, overall, follow the party where ever they may go.
