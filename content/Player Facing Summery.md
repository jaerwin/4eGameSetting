# Chapter 1 - Setting Out
Before the adventure began, the players were hired by [[Parle Cranewing]], a scholar looking into the various ruins across the land. They need to map out a ruin North of [[Winterhaven]]. The reward for this task is 250gp.

On their way to the town, the party were ambushed by a small band of kobolds. After dispatching them quickly, the party set off for town again. Once there, they learn from the local inn that their is an individual who's been terrorizing the town some, a man named Kalarel. It's not fully clear what he does yet, though some claim he's running a cult out of the old ruins. The inn keeper, [[Salvana Wrafton]], asks the party refrains from killing anyone in her inn.

The party explores the town a bit, purchasing some supplies from local blacksmith, and picked up that the kobolds they'd encountered have been ambushing travelers on the roads for weeks now. They get some info about both cult and kobolds from they mayor, [[Lord Padraig]], but it's not much more than they already know. They also recruit a scout who's seen the lair they are hiding in for himself. They all agree to head out in the morning.

On the path to the lair is another ambush. Once the players make quick work of these ones too, they search about the place some before moving on. The priestly kobold had an idol of sorts attached to a necklace. The idol is a dragon carved into obsidian, on the bottom is an etching of a skull with ram horns. This, as they remember, is an abstract depiction of [[Orcus]].

They carry on to the lair, and utilizing a bit of stealth manage to get the drop on the exterior guards, allowing the battle to progress rapidly. However, their luck ran out as they tried to sneak into the lair proper, as a botched roll caused the inside being alerted while the party posts up against the entrance.

They fought valiantly through the hordes of kobolds and slew their leader, a goblin named [[Irontooth]]. Once the fighting was done, they looted a magical [[Dwarven Chainmail]] and some much desired gold. Before leaving they were stopped by [[Kalarel the Vile]], who offered payment for the bodies that scatter the ground in their wake. The party rejected his offer, and took their leave for town once more.
