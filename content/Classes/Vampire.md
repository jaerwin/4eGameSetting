Stalkers of the night. Apex predators born from unholy meetings. There are a number of ways a vampire can come into being. Many are turned by a more powerful creature. A few of the earliest we turned by the gods themselves. And many still are the result of natural birth. Vampire mercenaries range in morality, as most have no true claim to power as their elders do. Few still shun their nature, seeing it as a curse upon the world; thus they actively choose fight against it and hunt their own kind.

# Playing a Vampire
To be a vampire to to naturally be a hunter of some kind. Their combat roles are not that different from a ranger or a rogue. Their personalities, however, range greatly. For the sake of party cohesion, it is advised that a player not choose an evil vampire and instead side at minimum close to the neutrality side of good and evil.

## Possible Motivations
1. Claim revenge against the one who turned you.
2. Uncover the mysterious of your past.
3. Hunt the other predators who stalk the night.
