When the great void gave way to the primordial deities, from their tongues came the first language. When their offspring came, through their creations came the first writings. Runepriests are the ancient arbiters of this now sacred language. Through their devotion, their gods gifted them with the power of runes, and thus the primordial forms of protection and destruction. With it, one may unravel the very methods of creation.

# Playing a Runepriest
Runepriests are devout followers of the gods. Sometimes focused on a single one, but not always. Usually, their gifts come from a god who's sphere includes knowledge, protection, or destruction. Playing as such a character requires one to form a relationship with said gods, though still very similar to clerics and paladins.

## Possible Motivations
1. Uncover the secrets of the gods.
2. Bring justice to the lands, casting out the evil at foot.
3. Building your own flock to keep safe in these troubling times.
