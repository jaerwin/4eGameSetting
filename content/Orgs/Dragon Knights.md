The dragon knights where a loyal regiment under [[Good King Cyrus]]. Their origins trace back to [[The Order of Bahamut]], an ancient organization of dragon worshipers who studied the beasts and their divine god in the ways of warfare. Now, their descendants are hunted by the dark lords who covet their heads, or their capabilities. None of those who descend from this holy order are in any way related to the evil goddess [[Tiamat]]. Rather, those who follow the wicked beast are eternal rivals. A member born of one order has an unnatural ability to sense when a member of the other is near them.
