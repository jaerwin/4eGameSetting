The Dark Powers and an unknown force that exists within the [[Shadowfell]]. They are responsible for trapping the [[Dread Lords]] within the demiplane. They created what scholars refer to as [[The Domains of Dread]], terrible lands ruled by vile monsters. These days, it seems as if these planes have in many ways merged with our own. Following the [[Cataclysm]], much of the [[Shadowfell]] has taken root in the mortal realms.
